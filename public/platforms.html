About

TennisExplore is a retrieval system over Tennis Australia's research library, presentations, video and match records. Every answer is traced to the document, page or timestamp it came from, and access is filtered by role before anything is retrieved. When the knowledge base cannot answer a question, it says so rather than guessing.
